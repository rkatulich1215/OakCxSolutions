QA/QC

Quality control tied to commissioning readiness.

A quality issue found at Level 3 costs weeks. Found at Level 1 or 2, it costs hours. We run independent QA/QC for owners, tenants, and general contractors, built around one goal: systems that are ready when the Commissioning Agent shows up.

This is not general inspection. Every check traces back to what the CxA will test, so quality findings and readiness status live in one picture instead of two.

What QA/QC covers

  • Installation verification against approved submittals and design documents
  • Deficiency tracking from discovery through verified closure
  • Pre-functional readiness reviews before Level 3 testing starts
  • Cross-system quality checks across MEP, controls, and life safety
  • Weekly quality reporting with open items, owners, and due dates

How we work

From first walk to Level 4 handoff.
